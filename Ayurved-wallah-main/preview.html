1. HARITAKI (Terminalia chebula - Combretaceae)

Gana: Prajasthapana, Jvaraghna (Charaka); Parushakadi (Sushruta)

Rasa: Kashaya pradhana (Pancha Rasa except Lavana)

Veerya: Ushna | Vipaka: Madhura | Guna: Laghu, Ruksha

Classical Shloka: "यस्य माता गृहे नास्ति तस्य माता हरीतकी..."

💡 High-Yield Exam Mnemonic: Haritaki lacks Lavana (Salt). If asked which Rasa is absent in Haritaki, immediately select Lavana.
